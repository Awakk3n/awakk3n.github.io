## Protocol

1. **Check before creating.** Before a chapter introduces a framework, search this registry. Reuse or extend an existing entry rather than creating a near-duplicate.
2. **Tag provenance.** `Established` (cite the origin) or `Original` (created for this book).
3. **Six required parts:** inputs, outputs, assumptions, tradeoffs, failure modes, one worked example. A framework missing any part is not registered.
4. **ID format:** `FW-CNN-NN`.
5. **One owner.** Other chapters reference the ID and may add examples, not new versions.
6. **If two frameworks overlap,** record the relationship in `Related` and decide in the revision queue whether to merge.
7. **Core flag (A212).** The framework cards marked core in the chapters (a line `Core framework.` under the heading) are the must-know set; this registry shows the flag and lists them. Nothing is deleted.
