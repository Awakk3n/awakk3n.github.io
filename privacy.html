PRIVACY · YOUTUBE

Your data stays
with you.

What this site keeps in your browser, what it never collects, and how chapter audio uses YouTube.

What this site stores

Everything below is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent to a server: your theme choice, reading progress, bookmarks, chapter notes, practice scores, reading text size, and audio resume positions. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no accounts. You can export or import your learning data on the Study desk, and clear everything through your browser’s site-data settings.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard request data (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement. Fonts are served from this site, so no font provider is contacted.

Chapter audio and YouTube

Some chapters have audio that plays through YouTube’s embedded player. This site uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube IFrame Player API) for that. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press Play audio. After that, YouTube loads its player and may collect data, set cookies or similar technology on your device, and show ads, under its own policies. This site does not receive that data.

By playing audio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The audio keeps playing only while this page is open and the player is on screen. Mobile browsers pause embedded players when the screen locks or you switch apps, and this site does not work around that.
